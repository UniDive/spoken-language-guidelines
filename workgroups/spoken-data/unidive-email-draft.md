# UniDive group email draft

Subject: UniDive spoken-language UD guidelines — status and next steps

Dear all,

A quick update from [WG1 Task 1.5](https://unidive.lisn.upsaclay.fr/doku.php?id=wg1:wg1) (spoken language guidelines): over the past months we've been drafting guidelines on spoken-specific annotation — segmentation into maximal units, tokenization/morphology for speech phenomena (numbers, acronyms, onomatopoeias, pauses, non-verbal behaviour...), syntax of co-constructions and other spoken-specific constructions, and standardized metadata naming, plus a proposal for sharing repeated metadata across a corpus without duplicating it on every sentence.

A few pointers:

- **We're turning this into a standing working group**, meeting before every UD release. Join the mailing list [here](TBA).
- **Open issues on the [UD docs repo](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/docs)** — weigh in if relevant: [#1273](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/docs/issues/1273) (segmentation), [#1289](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/docs/issues/1289) (tokenization/morphology), [#1280](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/docs/issues/1280) (co-constructions), [#1290](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/docs/issues/1290) (spoken-specific constructions), [#1282](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/docs/issues/1282) (treebank structure/metadata sharing), [#1300](https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/docs/issues/1300) (metadata standardization).
- **Everything is documented** in [UniDive/spoken-language-guidelines](https://github.com/UniDive/spoken-language-guidelines) (rendered at [grew.fr/spoken-language-guidelines](https://grew.fr/spoken-language-guidelines/workgroups/spoken-data/)), including the full treebank survey table and per-treebank suggestions: [ud_spoken_treebanks.html](https://grew.fr/spoken-language-guidelines/workgroups/spoken-data/ud_spoken_treebanks.html). Note the treebank comparison was done semi-automatically with Claude's (Anthropic) help, so corrections/pushback are welcome.

Get involved however suits you — commenting on the issues, reacting to a treebank suggestion, or just joining future meetings.
