## Modality identification

**Is spoken part clearly identifiable?** No - despite `genre` listing `spoken`, the README indicates the 100 sentences are written: they're manually translated (not transcribed) short conversational tourism-related texts from the MADAR Parallel Corpus, itself derived from the written Basic Traveling Expression Corpus (BTEC). There's no indication any sentence is a transcription of actual speech - flagged to maintainers to confirm or drop `spoken` from `genre`.
